FOUNDER PHOTOS: paste your own cropped photos here
==================================================
Replace these two files (keep the EXACT names, lowercase, .jpg):

  towsif-khan.jpg      <- Towsif Khan
  mesbah-hasin.jpg     <- Mesbah Hasin

That is all. These two files are used on every page automatically:
Home (Meet the founders), Team, About, both founder pages and the Gallery.

Tips
- Best crop: portrait 4:5 or 3:4 (for example 1000 x 1250 px), face in the upper half.
- Keep each file under 500 KB (save as JPG, quality 80-85).
- Small avatar tiles and the wide Home panel crop the photo automatically around the upper part, so leave a little space above the head.
